About IRR Calculation

The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) shown here is calculated using the Newton-Raphson method to find the discount rate that makes the Net Present Value (NPV) of all cash flows equal to zero. This includes your initial investment (negative cash flow at Year 0), annual operating cash flows, and your final proceeds at exit. The levered IRR accounts for debt financing, while the unlevered IRR assumes an all-cash purchase. IRR is time-weighted, making it more accurate than simple ROI for comparing investments with different holding periods.
